# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 3, 2026**

Welcome to **Talawire**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our website and services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** When you register or log in using Google OAuth or email, we collect basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture.
- **Application Data:** We collect and store data created within the application, including mindmaps, project boards, nodes, and associated media files.
- **Usage Data:** We may collect non-personal analytics data regarding how you interact with our service to improve performance and user experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Talawire application.
- Authenticate your account and secure your access.
- Enable mindmap sharing and collaboration features as requested by you.
- Improve and personalize our features and services.

---

## 3. Google OAuth Data Usage

Talawire's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including the Limited Use requirements.
- We do not sell your Google user data to third parties.
- We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements.
- We only access the minimum necessary scopes required for user authentication and authorization.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Protection

- **Data Security:** We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal information and project data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.
- **Third-Party Services:** We do not share your private data with third parties except as necessary to provide our core service infrastructure (e.g., database hosting, cloud processing) or when legally required.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Data Controls

You have full control over your account data:
- You can update or delete your account at any time through your Profile settings.
- You can delete individual mindmaps and projects at any time.

---

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at `support@talawire.com`.
